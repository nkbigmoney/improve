My Stats

Log where you are now and where you're aiming for, per game.

Why this is manual: there isn't a way to pull your live rank/stats from tracker.gg or Overwolf into a third-party site. Riot, Valve, Blizzard, and Marvel Rivals don't publish a public stats API, and tracker.gg's data is only accessible through a paid, approved partner integration — not something a personal site can just plug into. So instead of a broken "connect your tracker" button, this is a spot to enter your numbers yourself and keep a goal next to them. If that changes later, this section is built so a real data source can slot in without changing the layout.
